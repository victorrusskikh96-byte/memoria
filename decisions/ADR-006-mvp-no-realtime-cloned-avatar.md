# ADR-006: MVP excludes real-time cloned voice and video

Status: Accepted for v0.1

## Decision

The first product ships capture, timeline, evidence-backed text interaction, family permissions, export and succession planning. Real-time cloned voice and video require a later safety and consent gate.

## Consequences

The MVP tests durable value with lower biometric, moderation and platform risk. Original recordings provide emotional authenticity from day one.
