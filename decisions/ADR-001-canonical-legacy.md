# ADR-001: One verified human, one canonical Legacy

Status: Accepted for v0.1

## Decision

Memoria links one canonical Legacy to one verified human. Other family-created profiles remain collections or reconstructed records until the subject verifies and claims them, or the succession process recognizes them as a posthumous archive.

## Consequences

Duplicate detection, merge review and visible provenance are required. The rule reduces impersonation and fragmented identity but increases verification complexity.
