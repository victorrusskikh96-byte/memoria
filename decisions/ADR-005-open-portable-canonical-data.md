# ADR-005: Canonical data remains open and portable

Status: Accepted for v0.1

## Decision

The Legacy Package will contain original media, transcripts, structured memories, snapshots, relationships, consent and provenance in documented formats. Embeddings and caches are derived infrastructure.

## Consequences

The export can survive model changes and service closure. Initial design will evaluate BagIt, JSON-LD, W3C PROV and C2PA-compatible provenance.
