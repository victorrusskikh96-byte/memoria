# Glossary v0.1

- **Canonical Legacy**: the authoritative, creator-bound collection of original evidence, structured memories, snapshots, relationships, permissions and provenance.
- **Creator**: the living person creating their own Legacy.
- **Subject**: the person represented by a piece of data. The creator and subject are often, but not always, the same person.
- **Capture**: a recording, interview, upload or structured statement added to a Legacy.
- **Life Snapshot**: an immutable representation of a person during a defined life period.
- **Verified Snapshot**: a snapshot self-recorded during the relevant period under an authenticated session.
- **Reconstructed Snapshot**: a later reconstruction based on archives or third-party accounts.
- **Evidence**: an original or derived item supporting a statement.
- **Provenance**: information describing where an item came from, who created it, when, and how it changed.
- **Consent Receipt**: a versioned record of permission with purpose, scope, data classes, recipients, duration and revocation rules.
- **Steward**: a person allowed to manage specified operational tasks without gaining ownership of the represented identity.
- **Successor Trustee**: a preselected steward whose authority may activate after incapacity or death.
- **Memorial State**: the posthumous operating state in which Verified Self is locked and only permitted stewardship actions remain.
- **Memory Mode**: AI responses limited to claims reasonably supported by Legacy evidence.
- **Reflection Mode**: clearly labeled extrapolation based on documented values and patterns.
- **Legacy Package**: a portable export containing canonical data and integrity manifests.
