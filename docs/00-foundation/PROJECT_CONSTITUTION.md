# Memoria Project Constitution v0.1

## Purpose

Memoria helps a person preserve a truthful, evolving and portable digital legacy for future generations. AI guides people to original human evidence. It does not replace the human being or invent a more convenient version of them.

## Constitutional principles

1. Human first, AI second.
2. One verified human has one canonical Legacy.
3. Private by default.
4. The creator controls their Legacy while capable and alive.
5. Consent is explicit, granular, informed and revocable.
6. Original human evidence is more authoritative than AI generation.
7. Inference is never presented as memory.
8. Human identity is temporal. Memoria preserves evolution instead of overwriting the past.
9. A Digital Legacy is a sequence of authentic life states, not a static persona.
10. After death, Verified Self cannot be silently rewritten.
11. Every Legacy is portable outside Memoria.
12. Engagement must come from meaning and trust, never emotional manipulation.
13. Delete, export, privacy and safety controls cannot depend on a premium plan.
14. Memoria never promises storage forever. It promises portability, transparent preservation terms and recoverable exit paths.

## Product boundaries

Memoria will not:

- create a public avatar marketplace in the initial product;
- sell personal or biometric data;
- train general-purpose models on private Legacy content by default;
- use guilt, jealousy, dependency or simulated need to increase engagement;
- let a trustee own or rewrite the identity of a deceased person;
- present generated content as a direct recording or verified statement;
- infer consent for voice or likeness cloning from a generic terms-of-service acceptance.

## Decision test

Every feature must answer four questions before implementation:

1. Whose identity does it affect?
2. Which consent authorizes it?
3. Who controls it now and after incapacity or death?
4. Can the result be exported, audited, corrected and safely stopped?

A feature that cannot answer these questions does not enter development.
