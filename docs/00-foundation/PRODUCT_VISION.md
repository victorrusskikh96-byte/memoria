# Product Vision v0.1

## Definition

Memoria is a privacy-first Digital Human Legacy Platform that allows a person to preserve an authentic, portable and consent-based representation of their memories, personality, voice, relationships and life story for future generations.

## Core user promise

Memoria preserves the person behind the files: what happened, what it meant, how they changed, who mattered and what evidence supports each answer.

## Initial experience

1. A creator opens a private Legacy and establishes identity.
2. A 15-minute adaptive interview creates the first self-recorded snapshot.
3. Gentle prompts add memories over time.
4. Life Chapters record changes in appearance, voice, values and relationships.
5. Family members receive only the access the creator explicitly grants.
6. AI answers in Memory Mode or Reflection Mode and reveals its evidence level.
7. Original recordings remain one tap away.
8. The creator can export a complete, machine-readable Legacy Package.

## Product wedge

The first release focuses on living creators and their families. It provides guided interviews, original-media capture, temporal snapshots, evidence-backed retrieval, family sharing, export and a basic succession plan. Real-time cloned voice and video avatars remain outside the first MVP.

## Long-term outcome

A descendant can ask, "What was my great-grandfather like when he was my age?" Memoria returns the correct life period, real recordings, documented relationships and clearly marked interpretation without pretending that a generated answer is a historical fact.
