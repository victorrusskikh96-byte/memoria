# Ownership and Stewardship v0.1

## Goal

Separate control of the Legacy, ownership of media, privacy interests and operational stewardship.

## Rules

1. The verified creator controls the canonical Legacy while capable and alive.
2. Uploading content does not erase the rights of photographers, speakers, correspondents or people depicted.
3. Consent grants permission for a purpose; it does not sell or transfer identity.
4. Memoria receives only the limited license needed to provide the selected service.
5. Memoria does not sell private Legacy data or use it for advertising.
6. General-purpose model training is opt-in and separated from service operation. The MVP should omit it entirely.
7. Trustees receive scoped duties, never ownership of the person's voice, face, name or personality.
8. AI outputs remain traceable derivatives. They never replace source evidence.

## Multi-person memories

Each story may have a primary narrator, represented people, contributors and rights holders. Access decisions must account for all of them. When claims conflict, the system can restrict the item without deleting the underlying evidence until the dispute is resolved.

## Portability

The creator can export canonical content in a documented, machine-readable package. Export includes originals and provenance. Proprietary embeddings are optional derivatives and never the only usable representation.

## Commercial limits

Delete, export, privacy and safety controls stay free. Paid plans may charge for storage, capture assistance, preservation services, premium generation and family administration, but never for recovering a user's own canonical data.
