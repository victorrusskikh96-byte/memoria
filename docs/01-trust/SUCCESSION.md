# Succession v0.1

## Goal

Carry out the creator's instructions after incapacity or death while preventing impersonation, hostile takeover and silent historical revision.

## Plan contents

- primary and backup trustees;
- activation evidence and review path;
- people who must be notified;
- optional dual-control threshold;
- access matrix by person and data class;
- future messages with original recording date;
- permissions for text, voice, image and video generation;
- retention, export, archival and deletion instructions;
- dispute and trustee-replacement rules;
- jurisdiction and review date.

## Activation sequence

1. Receive a report.
2. Enter Safeguard Hold.
3. Verify claimant identity.
4. Validate evidence and plan version.
5. Notify designated parties.
6. Apply the cooling period.
7. Resolve objections or enter Disputed state.
8. Activate Memorial state and issue scoped authority grants.

## Trustee duties

Trustees preserve integrity, execute instructions, protect privacy and document actions. They cannot expand their own permissions, impersonate the creator, rewrite Verified Self or authorize a use the creator prohibited.

## Incapacity

Incapacity and death are separate triggers. Temporary incapacity should favor reversible support and recovery. The product must allow restoration of full creator control after recovery.

## Future messages

Only human-authored or explicitly approved messages may be scheduled as messages from the creator. The interface shows the original recording or approval date. The system never invents "what they wanted to say."

## Disputes

Conflicting trustee claims, contested death evidence, suspected coercion, family takedown requests or court orders place affected operations in a reversible freeze. Emergency privacy restrictions may apply immediately; irreversible deletion or public disclosure waits for resolution.
